# Idea 0001 — Open-ended organism and ecosystem extensions

**Status:** deferred until M0–M2 are implemented and evaluated.
**Origin:** initial Surlife brainstorming.
**Why preserve:** these motivations should inform extension contracts, but building them all in v0 would make failures uninterpretable.

## Health, age and self-repair
Organisms could accumulate illness states, grow older, trade off repair against reproduction, and develop inherited backups/tests/diagnostics. Avoid literal corruption of arbitrary source code as a v0 mechanic. **First test:** compare lineages allowed to invest in an evolvable error-checking skill against a fixed safety baseline.

## Reproduction and inherited skills
Asexual copying, two-parent combination of compatible code modules, selective memory inheritance and virtual-capital splits. **First test:** demonstrate inheritance of an individually verified skill across process restarts and a child-run benchmark, not merely a copied prompt.

## Assimilation
Defeated organisms may transfer remaining virtual energy and licensed/public skills; acquiring a module has possible integration/testing cost. **First test:** verify acquired module helps on held-out tasks rather than contaminating evaluation data.

## Different ancestors
Initially different souls on the same shared player core, later distinct starting strategy implementations. **First test:** measure whether ancestry diversity matters after controlling initial token budgets and task advantage.

## Social observation, imitation and secrecy
Agents may buy access to rival public action histories and voluntarily published modules, trade skills and copy techniques. **First test:** social-learning vs no-social-learning ablation with the same opponents and matched information costs.

## Indirect environmental competition
An organism pays for drought, fog, market shifts or legally proposed new environmental effects; effects apply to every actor including sponsor. **First test:** show interventions alter strategy choices and competitive ranking in a seeded world without violating ledger/physics contracts.

## Evolvable metabolism and ways of learning
Agent-written strategies may optimize storage, memory compression, model selection, skill invocation, repair and resource allocation. The environment's externally enforced accounting and executable isolation remain unchanged. **First test:** compare actual token efficiency before and after adoption of a measured, versioned optimization.

## Distinct identity or 'soul'
A versioned initial identity prompt can become an evolved personal strategy through experience. It must never be promoted to a privileged system instruction. **First test:** evaluate behavioral and strategy divergence from identical code with differing initial souls across multiple random seeds.

## Philosophical caution
Words like soul, jealousy, illness and death describe in-world mechanics/observed behavior, not evidence of subjective experience. Avoid anthropomorphic claims as measurements.

## Gate to promote any idea
Promote to a specific PLAN milestone only with a small interface proposal, known resource price, independent evaluation method, failure conditions and an explicit maintainer decision. Otherwise keep deferred.

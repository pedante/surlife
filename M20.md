# Surlife M2.0 — Persistent Shared-Resource World

**Opt-in, deterministic, offline only:** `surlife --m20`.

M2.0 is Surlife's first persistent ecology experiment. It is deliberately
a **new versioned referee** (`m20-v1`), not a silent edit to the historical
M0-v2 puzzle arena or M1.4–M1.11 cognition/scoring experiments.

## What changed?

In the older benchmarks, every tick generated a new independent problem.
A mistake hurt this tick's payout, but the physical challenge next tick was
unchanged.

**Now agents share a resource patch.** Decisions alter its state:

- **Stock**, initially 8 units, is a COMMON pool (maximum 14).
  Harvesting takes units away; another organism can't also consume them.
- **Soil**, initially fertility 4 (maximum 8), determines future regrowth.
  A high-intensity harvest damages soil when at least two units were
  obtained. A cultivation action improves it, but costs energy now.
- **Weather** is generated from seed and tick alone, before decisions,
  with normal/rain/drought. Weather changes the amount of regrowth
  and drought also reduces fertility.
- **Regrowth occurs AFTER actions**, so an organism cannot harvest
  something it has just made grow in the same tick.
- **Death is absorbing.** Zero-energy organisms cannot act and cannot
  become alive merely because the climate improves.

This means today’s extraction can destroy tomorrow’s opportunities,
while the benefit of cultivation is delayed and shared with rivals.

## Bounded environmental actions

| Organism action | Effort energy | Effect |
|---|---:|---|
| `rest` | 0 | Conserves effort (still pays upkeep) |
| `harvest1` | 0 | Requests one resource, gains 3 energy if obtained |
| `harvest3` | 1 | Requests up to three resources, gains 2 energy per allocated unit; soil loses 1 if 2+ obtained |
| `tend` | 2 | Restores two soil fertility (up to 8); helps later regrowth |

Each **alive** organism also pays 1 energy of upkeep per tick. All fees
and rewards are separate transfers in the virtual-energy ledger,
including a `world` balancing account whose balance may be negative.
The sum of balances remains precisely zero; soil and stock are PHYSICAL
environmental state, not additional virtual currency.

Actions are simultaneous: both choices are collected from a snapshot,
then preflight-validated before changing any balance or ecology. Under
scarcity, stock units are allocated one at a time using an alternating
priority set by seed and tick, not Python dictionary order.

No opponent private energy, future weather, exact reward optimizer, API
keys or direct referee mutation is included in a policy observation.

## First four trusted organisms

- `extractive`: favors aggressive harvesting while the patch has stock
- `steward`: pays to restore low-fertility soil and otherwise harvests lightly
- `opportunist`: alternates between heavy and light harvest depending on stock
- `rest`: never harvests or repairs the patch

These are hard-coded experimental policies, **not LLMs or learned
organisms**. The environmental dynamics are nontrivial but this is
still a *small toy ecology*, not an open-ended artificial ecosystem.

No real model calls. There is **no monthly quota, Google billing, or
provider token use**. Output is a bounded, versioned, independently
replayable JSON report. Use a new output filename for each experiment.

### Controls and reporting

Each run contains **two episodes** under the same deterministic seed
and weather sequence: A=steward/B=extractive and the *mirrored*
A=extractive/B=steward seating assignment. This controls for
alternating resource-priority asymmetries (but does not establish
statistical generalization).

Reported measures include final virtual energy, organism survival
and number of alive ticks, harvested units per organism, cultivation
work, remaining stock/fertility, stock-empty ticks and total regrowth.
The verifier reconstructs both ecology episodes, every transfer and
every action from trusted code. SHA-256 report heads detect accidental
or inconsistent changes, not an authenticated publisher identity.

M2.0 has *no* arbitrary executable model code, no OS/network surface,
no arbitrary actions, no unbounded simulation, no mutation of the
historical M0 referee and no changes to organism prompt memory.

## Next step toward artificial life

**M2.1** should make ecological interactions themselves adaptive:
allow organisms to observe previous seasons (through bounded memory),
choose authorized interventions, and compete/cooperate under matched
ecology and budgets. Before allowing LLMs or imported agent frameworks,
version the new action schema, add input-admissibility constraints,
compare against extractive/steward/opportunist controls, and enforce
host-only energy/referee state.

Longer-term M2 experiments should test cumulative inheritance and
whether observed adaptation is robust over held-out seeds and changed
climates, not just successful harvesting in one friendly world.
